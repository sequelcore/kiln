# Kiln Research Synthesis

## Purpose

This document states the consolidated research conclusion for Kiln.

It explains the intellectual basis behind the architecture, but it is not the
architecture source of truth. For doctrine, use `docs/architecture/`.

## Core Conclusion

Kiln should be treated as a biocybernetic control plane:

- cybernetics provides the governing control vocabulary
- biological and neural research provide selected mechanism lineage
- explicit contracts outrank metaphor in every case

The research does not justify a literal-organism model, metaphor-first product
language, or uncontrolled emergence as an engineering strategy.

## Mechanism Families That Survived Consolidation

### Primary

- cybernetic control loops
- homeostasis and allostasis
- layered memory
- layered safety and threat memory
- threshold-based and stigmergic coordination

### Secondary

- nervous-system routing and gating
- attention and salience bottlenecks
- reconsolidation
- bounded differentiation and inhibition

### Long-Range Inspiration Only

- fungal or mycelial substrate models

These ideas remain useful only when they produce explicit software behavior,
policy, ownership, telemetry, or failure modes.

## What The Research Validates

The research most strongly supports:

- fast-path gating before expensive deliberation
- explicit memory layers with different retention and mutation rules
- context as a constrained attentional bottleneck
- explicit operating modes and regulation horizons
- layered safety with threat-memory behavior
- shared-state coordination with thresholds, decay, and inhibition
- quorum or latch behavior for stable coordination transitions
- observable correction loops rather than hidden corrective magic

## Priority Ranking

The research stack is not flat. The most useful ordering is:

1. cybernetic control loops as the governing frame
2. homeostasis and allostasis for regulation and operating modes
3. layered memory and reconsolidation
4. layered safety with danger signals and threat memory
5. context governance as attentional bottleneck management
6. shared-state coordination with thresholds, decay, and inhibition

Nervous-system routing, fungal substrate models, and morphogenesis are useful
only when they sharpen one of those six areas rather than generating a parallel
identity layer.

## What The Research Rejects

The research rejects:

- literal-organism framing
- biology as branding without mechanism
- vague emergence language in place of control design
- subsystem naming that hides real ownership
- analogies that erase where software differs from biology

## Architectural Consequence

The architecture consequence is straightforward:

- Kiln should be described as a control plane
- controllers, governors, registries, and safety boundaries should remain
  explicit
- memory, coordination, and adaptation should be documented as governed
  subsystems rather than as loose inspiration
- biological and neural references should support architecture, not compete
  with it

The research layer therefore needs both:

- a small root synthesis set for orientation
- a small number of topical research docs where memory, safety, regulation,
  and coordination are developed without leaking back into doctrine

## Open Pressure Points

The research still leaves a small set of pressure points that architecture must
handle directly:

- exact layer defaults in safety
- predictive regulation versus purely reactive control
- explicit shared-medium rules in coordination and context
- reconsolidation rules based on confidence, provenance, and topic coherence
- quorum and latch behavior in coordination transitions
- trust-boundary ownership for tool execution and approvals

## Consolidation Rule

Research material should be consolidated into the active documents under
`docs/research/`.

Git history is sufficient for superseded drafts. The canonical tree should not
depend on an archive directory to remain understandable.
