# Kiln Research Synthesis

## Purpose

This document synthesizes the biological, neurological, and cybernetic
research program into a single research narrative for Kiln.

It is not the architecture doctrine. It is the intellectual basis that informs
the architecture.

## Core Conclusion

The research supports treating Kiln as a biocybernetic control plane: a
cybernetic control architecture informed by selected biological and neural
mechanisms.

The strongest result is not a literal organism model. It is a disciplined
architectural synthesis whose mechanisms are drawn from:

- layered memory
- attention and salience bottlenecks
- homeostatic and allostatic regulation
- immune-style layered defense
- threshold-based coordination
- active shared-medium effects
- bounded differentiation and inhibition

The control-plane contract layer remains primary. The biological and neural
mechanisms are first-class lineage for the architecture, naming, and product
framing when they remain grounded in explicit behavior.

## Mechanism Ranking

Strongest mechanism families for Kiln:

1. cybernetic control loops
2. homeostasis and allostasis
3. layered memory
4. immune-style defense
5. threshold and stigmergic coordination

Useful but secondary:

- nervous-system routing and gating
- attention and salience
- reconsolidation
- morphogenesis and differentiation

Useful mainly as long-term inspiration:

- fungal or mycelial substrate models

## What The Research Validates

The research strongly validates:

- fast-path gating before expensive deliberation
- explicit memory layers with different retention policies
- context as an attentional bottleneck
- explicit operational modes and regulation
- layered safety with threat memory
- threshold-based coordination with decay and inhibition
- active shared medium as a real design concern
- quorum-style commit and latch behavior for phase transitions and coordinated
  shifts in behavior

## What The Research Rejects

The research rejects:

- literal-organism branding
- metaphor-first branding detached from mechanism
- vague organism language
- uncontrolled emergence as an engineering virtue
- metaphor without mechanism
- analogy that hides where the software model differs from biology

## The Architecture Consequence

The architecture consequence is clear:

- Kiln should be described and built as a biocybernetic control plane
- architecture should revolve around governors, control loops, memory policy,
  safety layers, adaptation, and observable corrections
- downstream product surfaces should not redefine Kiln identity
- neurotech and cyberpunk framing are valid only when tied back to these
  mechanism families and the control-plane contract layer

## Open Research Pressure Points

The research still leaves a few areas that require disciplined architectural
handling:

- exact safety default behavior by layer
- predictive regulation vs purely reactive control
- active shared-medium design in current architecture, not only future
  distributed infrastructure
- reconsolidation rules based on confidence, provenance, and topic coherence
- quorum and latch behavior in coordination

## Coordination Research Consequence

The strongest coordination takeaway is not "multi-agent" in the abstract. It is
that Kiln should prefer shared-state, threshold-based, stigmergic coordination
over chatty direct agent-to-agent choreography whenever the task allows it.

This includes:

- threshold-driven allocation
- shared task state
- decay and reinforcement
- inhibition to prevent duplicate work
- quorum or latch conditions for stable transition into a new coordination
  phase

The old research on coordination intelligence and the quorum/biofilm mapping
both point in this direction.
